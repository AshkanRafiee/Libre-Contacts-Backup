Added:
- Persian, French, German, Turkish, Spanish, Chinese, and Arabic translations, alongside English
- The app now follows your device's language automatically; a "Language" link in the footer lets you override it per-app at any time
- Right-to-left layout support for Persian and Arabic

Fixed:
- F-Droid builds of this app now reproduce byte-for-byte identical to the binary published on GitHub (a metadata file AGP embeds only when a .git folder is present was breaking the comparison, not the app itself)
- Non-English layouts no longer need to scroll to see content that fits on one screen in English

Reminder: v2.2.0 raised the password-based backup encryption strength. An ENCRYPTED backup made with v2.2.0 or later cannot be opened by v2.1.1 or earlier, and vice versa — use the app version that created it to restore it. Unencrypted backups are unaffected.
