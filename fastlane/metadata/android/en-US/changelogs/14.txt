Changes:
- Fixed contacts being split into duplicate entries when backing up: a contact aggregated from multiple raw contacts (e.g. Google + WhatsApp) is now exported as a single vCard
- Fixed restoring the same backup twice creating duplicate contacts; same-name cards now merge into the existing contact
- Merging no longer duplicates phones, emails, addresses, events, relations, nicknames, notes, websites or IM handles, and fills in empty fields of the existing contact
- Multiple nicknames and notes per contact are now preserved instead of keeping only the last one
- Backup and restore now handle phone numbers that differ only by spacing/case without creating duplicates
- Test suite portability fixes for modern adb and Android releases
