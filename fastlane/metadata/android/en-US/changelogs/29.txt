Fixed:
- Restoring a backup no longer crashes on Android 10 and 11, where the phone lacks the newer SIM contacts provider
- Automatic backups can no longer be cancelled silently by an interrupted or overlapping run. If a backup is stopped partway through (slow phone, Doze, or the system reclaiming resources), the next scheduled backup still happens at the usual time
- A slow-to-read SIM card phonebook can no longer delay or derail an automatic backup
- After a device reboot, the automatic schedule restores itself on the first app launch
Changed:
- When an automatic backup is skipped or fails, the reason now also shows on the main screen — not only in a notification that is easy to miss
