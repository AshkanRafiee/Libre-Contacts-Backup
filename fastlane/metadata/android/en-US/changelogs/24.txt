Added:
- Weekly and monthly scheduled backups, alongside the existing daily option: pick a day of the week or a day of the month and a time, and the backup runs automatically in the background
- When a month is shorter than the scheduled day-of-month, the monthly backup runs on that month's last day instead
- The schedule menu now shows the currently scheduled backup at the top, so you can always see what is set even when the main screen cannot fit the full label
