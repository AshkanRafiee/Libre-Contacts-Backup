Changes:
- Contact groups/labels are now restored instead of being dropped
- Two different people who happen to share the same name are no longer merged into one contact on restore
- Postal addresses no longer restore with the street and PO box swapped
- Restoring a contact assembled from multiple accounts (e.g. Google + a local contact) now merges them correctly instead of leaving one behind
- Fixed the restore completion message repeating itself ("Restore complete: Restore complete...")
- Fixed a white flash on the loading screen on Android 12+; it's dark now, matching the rest of the app
- General reliability improvements to backup and restore, verified against datasets of up to 500 contacts
