Libre Contacts Backup is a FLOSS native Android app for private, local-first contact backups.

Features:
* Scheduled daily, weekly, monthly, and custom-time backups
* Single-file LCB archives containing VCF, JSON, and CSV data
* Optional password-protected encryption
* Restore with visible progress
* Manual CSV, VCF, and Excel-compatible exports
* User-controlled retention for automatic backup archives
