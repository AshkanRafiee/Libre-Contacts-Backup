Libre Contacts Backup is a FLOSS native Android app for private, local-first contact backups.

Features:
* Lossless backup preserving the full Contact → RawContact → Data hierarchy
* Scheduled daily, weekly, monthly, and custom-time backups
* Single-file LCB archives containing canonical JSON, VCF, CSV, and normalized exports
* Optional AES-256-GCM password-protected encryption (Android Keystore backed)
* Restore with per-contact progress and automatic raw contact merging
* Manual CSV, VCF, and Excel-compatible exports
* User-controlled retention for automatic backup archives
