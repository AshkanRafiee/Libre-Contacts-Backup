Back up and restore your Android contacts without giving the app internet access. Libre Contacts Backup is free and open source, with no account, ads, analytics, or tracking.

• Back up on your schedule. Pick a time for daily, weekly, or monthly backups, or back up manually whenever you need to.
• Control what stays. Keep the newest backup sets, keep them all, or keep the newest available backup for each day, week, and month across windows you choose (for example, the last 7 days, 4 weeks, and 3 months). The app trims older sets after a successful backup.
• Protect new backups with a password. Optional AES-256-GCM encryption is off by default. Keep the original password to restore an encrypted backup. Standalone CSV and vCard exports are unencrypted and are not removed by retention.
• Restore what you need. Backups preserve more than a standard vCard, including photos, groups, and account information where supported. Choose what to restore without changing the archive. SIM phonebook support depends on the device.

Choose a local folder to work entirely offline. The app has no internet permission and does not upload files. If you select a cloud-backed folder, its provider may sync the files. Android’s alarm and battery settings can affect scheduled backups.
